## Standard version (application questions, longer summaries)

I'm a production and materials planner with 10 years of planning experience in manufacturing. I
started as a Planner/Scheduler at Feniex Industries, where I built production schedules for
electro-mechanical vehicle lightbars made from both purchased and custom-manufactured parts. I
then worked as a Production Planner at Thermon, where I scheduled engineered-to-order industrial
heating products.

Across those roles, I managed the master production schedule and net requirements, and planned
make-to-order, configure-to-order and make-to-stock items. I maintained bills of materials,
routings and operations, and I set up labor tracking so production hours were captured
accurately. I worked in Acumatica, Microsoft Dynamics 365 and Dynamics AX 2012, and used advanced
Excel and Velixo for reporting.

Much of my best work was hands-on in the planning system. I configured Acumatica's MRP and moved
shortage tracking from manual Excel into it. I traced recurring stockouts to a min/max setup
error and fixed it, built a live material shortage dashboard, and kept BOMs accurate through a
migration from Smartsheet to Acumatica work orders. That work cut production lead times from 2–4
weeks to 5–10 days, brought 72% of core SKUs up to their minimum stock levels, and hit the
three-week stock rebuild goal on 9 of 12 tracked SKUs.

I'm looking for an individual-contributor role in material planning, production planning or
ERP/MRP, where I can spend my time doing the planning work directly.
